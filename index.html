    Give Furniture a New Life

    Six customers bring you furniture they are unsure about. For each one, diagnose their situation at the 🤝 Customer desk and choose the best circular strategy:

    🛋️ Retain🔧 Repair🎨 Aesthetic refresh🧰 Adapt🧩 Reconfigure🔄 Repurpose

    The Decision Helper ranks the options from your answers and shows each option’s environmental, economic and social impacts with peer-reviewed evidence and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) it supports. Then collect and repair parts to complete the job.

    • Delivery: 15 points + 8 coins. Best-fit strategy: +10. Also-possible strategy: +4.
    • Accurate diagnosis (6–7 of 7 answers match the customer): +3. Only reused parts: +5.
    • Each strategy adds a small symbolic impact (Retain 0 → Repurpose 6). Buying adds 20, discarding 10.
    Want to use the tool for a real piece of furniture? Click 🧭 Decision Helper — it works without playing the game and gives a summary you can copy for a customer.

    📚 Evidence & SDGs

    🎨 Finish Studio

    Clean, sand and refinish the customer’s furniture. Required for an Aesthetic refresh; optional for any job to pick the delivered colour.

    Practical tip: choose water-based, low-VOC paints or natural oils — finishes affect indoor air quality (SDG 3.9).

    🔧 Free Repair Workshop

    Select a part from your bag and repair it to 100% condition.

    1. Select a damaged part from your bag. Its condition will be below 100%.
    2. Click “Repair Part for Free.”
    3. Wait until the part reaches 100%. Repeat for each damaged part, then click “Back to Map.”

    🛒 New Parts Store

    A new part is in 100% condition, costs 8 coins and adds 20 impact points. Using it removes the +5 reuse bonus for that order.

    🗑️ Trash Bin

    Select a part to discard. Each discarded part adds 10 impact points. You can close this window and keep the part for repair instead.
